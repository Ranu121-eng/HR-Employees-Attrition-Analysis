# HR-Employees-Attrition-Analysis
# 📌 Project Overview
Employee attrition can impact workforce stability, productivity, employee experience and organizational knowledge.
This project analyzes employee data to understand where attrition is concentrated and which employee characteristics are associated with employee turnover.
The analysis covers demographic, job-related, compensation, satisfaction, travel and tenure-related factors.
# 🎯 Business Problem
The organization is experiencing employee attrition and wants to understand the factors associated with employee turnover.<br>
The key business challenge is to move beyond simply knowing how many employees left and understand:
1.	What is the overall employee attrition rate?
2.	How many employees have left the organization?
3.	What percentage of employees are currently active?
4.	Which department has the highest attrition?
5.	Which job roles have the highest attrition?
6.	Does monthly income appear to be associated with attrition?
7.	Does overtime affect attrition?
8.	Does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
9.	Does environment satisfaction relate to attrition?
10.	Does work-life balance relate to attrition?
11.	Does attrition vary by years at company?
12.	Does age group show differences in attrition?
13.	Does marital status show differences in attrition?
14.	Does distance from home relate to attrition?
